# 00a · The Numbers

> Rebuild school math from wherever you stopped, up to the floor every later module stands on.

**Requires:** nothing · **Hours:** 80-150 from a 9th-grade level; under 20 if the course challenges pass cold · **Next:** [M1 · The Machine](../core/01-the-machine.md), with [00b](00b-the-terminal.md)

Runs in parallel with [00b · The Terminal](00b-the-terminal.md). Both gates open M1. Work on paper, without a calculator: the point is to make the arithmetic automatic, because M1 asks you to do it by hand in five minutes.

## Outcomes

- Work with fractions, negative numbers, order of operations and percentages without a calculator.
- Solve a linear equation and read a function f(x) from its formula and its graph.
- Move between exponent and logarithm form (2¹⁰ = 1024 ⇔ log₂ 1024 = 10) and estimate either one.
- Read basic notation: Σ, ∈, ⊆, ∀, ∃, f: A → B.
- Read a short proof and say what each line relies on.

Calculus and linear algebra aren't needed. Statistics isn't needed until M9, and then only hypothesis testing. Only the RF track needs calculus.

**How to study this alone.** Do every worked example yourself before reading its solution. Keep an error log: each problem you got wrong, what the mistake was. Redo the log a week later. Re-deriving beats re-reading.

## Gate · 2 hours, on paper, no calculator

1. Pass the Khan Academy Algebra 1 course challenge cold at ≥ 90%. It's randomised, so it can't be rehearsed.
2. Rewrite five expressions between exponent and logarithm form. Give log₂ of 2ⁿ for any n up to 64, and estimate log₁₀ of a seven-digit number to the nearest integer.
3. State in words what five lines of notation mean, drawn from Book of Proof ch. 1 exercises you haven't done.

**Fail if:** you use a calculator, or score under 90% on part 1.

**Unlocks:** with the 00b gate, M1.
