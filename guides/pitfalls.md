# Pitfalls: failure modes and their early symptoms

> Each one is named by what it looks like from the inside, then the symptom you'll see first, then the correction.

| Pitfall | Early symptom | Correction |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| **The Reader.** Reading the material instead of passing the gate. | A module's hours are spent and you haven't attempted the gate. | Attempt the gate cold now. A failed attempt tells you what to read; the reading never tells you that. |
| **The Foundationalist.** Descending forever before touching a target. | You've added a prerequisite the module doesn't list. | Pass the gate in front of you. The path already orders the foundations. |
| **The Rebuilder.** Rewriting your plan, notes system or tooling instead of working. | More commits to your setup than to your ship log. | Freeze the setup until the next gate. |
| **The Collector.** Certificates, courses and books as progress. | A purchase made before a free resource stalled you. | [Spending](spending.md): buy only on a stall. |
| **The Decompiler Crutch.** Reading pseudocode you couldn't produce. | You can't explain a function with the decompiler off (M3's fail line). | Disassembly only, until the M3 gate passes. |
| **The Tool Operator.** Running a technique you've never done by hand. | You can't say what the tool did, step by step. | Rule 7: three times by hand first. |
| **The Heap Stall.** M6's heap section feels endless. | Two weeks without a new how2heap technique working. | Normal and planned for. Drop to one technique per session, record the glibc version, and write up the one that failed. |
| **The Silent Learner.** Working without shipping. | Fourteen days, nothing public. | Rule 2. Ship the smallest true thing, including a failed gate attempt. |
| **The Lone Grader.** Passing your own gate generously. | You argued with a Fail if line. | You failed. Re-read the [gate protocol](../gates/README.md); post the attempt for critique. |
| **The List Hunter.** Picking targets someone called easy. | Your target came from a "beginner bugs" list. | M9's fail line. Write the dated selection note first. |
| **The Bounty Drift.** The web/cloud line eats the spine. | More than 20% of weekly hours on bounty work before the M7 gate. | Cap it. Income now costs you M7 later. |
| **The Overrun.** A module runs past its hour range. | Past the top of the range with milestones outstanding. | Expected for M2 and M7. Recalibrate every later estimate against your logged pace (M2 gate) rather than speeding up. |
