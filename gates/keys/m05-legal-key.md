# M5 legal gate: key

> Open after your written answers are committed. A match on "authorized or not" with the wrong basis is a fail for that scenario.

The standard is the curriculum's, not the most permissive reading of any one statute: no document, no programme scope and no ownership means not authorized. Jurisdictions differ on what is prosecuted; they don't differ on what you can defend.
