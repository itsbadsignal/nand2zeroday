# Spending: hardware, books, courses, certifications

> Free-first means every gate can be passed on free resources plus four books. Prices are indicative, October 2026; check before buying.

Buy when a free resource stalls you, never before. A purchase is not progress.

## Hardware

- **Any 64-bit computer that can run two VMs at once.** Every module runs in Linux VMs on it (00b onward).
- **ARM64 for M6-M7:** QEMU emulation is free and enough for the gates. A physical ARM64 board is optional.
- **Optional:** the Ben Eater 8-bit kit (~€300, M1 Alt). An MTE-capable phone only if you enter the mobile candidate track.

## Books

The four Core books, in the order you'll need them:

| Module | Book | ~€ |
|--------|------|----|
| M1 | *Code* (Petzold, 2nd ed.) | 30 |
| M3 | *Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective* (3rd ed., used) | 70 |
| M5 | *Serious Cryptography* (Aumasson, 2nd ed.) | 50 |
| M9 | *The Art of Software Security Assessment* (used) | 60 |

About €210 in total, spread over years. Every other book in the path is Alt or Ref: buy one only if its Core counterpart stalls you. Libraries and the publishers' own sales cover most of them.

## Certifications

None is required, and none closes a gate. Your public work is the credential (Principle 5). If an employer will pay for an exploit-development certification, take it after the M7 gate, as confirmation of what the gate already showed, not instead of it.
